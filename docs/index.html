Your browser remembers what you were doing. Even when you don’t.

One click sorts your open tabs into named groups by what you’re actually doing — not by site. Find any tab by describing it. Shelve a whole project and come back to a brief of where you left off.

Forty loose tabs, ten seconds later.

Groups by intent, not by domain

Ten Twitter tabs won’t collapse into one “entertainment” pile. The research thread goes to research; the essay goes to reading. Same site, different jobs, different groups.

Ask for the tab you can’t find

“The hotel under $200 a night.” “The LinkedIn profile of the Stanford founder.” Describe what you remember and Shelve jumps to it — out of twenty lookalikes.

Shelve it. Come back to a brief.

Done for the day? A shelved project closes its tabs behind an AI note of where you left off — prices, options, what was still unchecked. Resume reopens everything as a fresh group.

Bring your own key. We never see your tabs.

Shelve talks directly to the AI provider you choose — OpenAI, Anthropic, Gemini, or a local Ollama. There is no Shelve backend, no account, no analytics. Your API key stays in your browser. The source is public so you don’t have to take our word for it.
