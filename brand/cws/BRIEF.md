# Shelve — Chrome Web Store screenshots (1280×800)

Series of 5 marketing frames for the Chrome Web Store listing. Redesigned from the
ground up for v0.7.0. Rendered with GPT Image via Codex from this brief; the icon in
`brand/icon-master.png` is the brand mark and must be reused verbatim (never redrawn).

## Brand system (all frames)
- Mood: calm, precise, "your browser remembers". Premium desktop-software feel, not SaaS gradient soup.
- Palette: near-black navy `#0B0F17` backgrounds with soft graphite folds like the icon's backdrop;
  brushed-silver metallic accent (the icon); one warm off-white highlight `#F4EFE6` for headlines;
  Chrome tab-group colours (blue, red, yellow, green, pink, purple, cyan, orange) only inside UI mockups.
- Type: geometric sans (Inter / SF-like). Headline 56–64px semibold, tight tracking. Subhead 22–24px regular,
  65% opacity. Left-aligned copy block on the left ~40% of the frame; UI mockup on the right ~55%.
- UI mockups must look like a real Chrome extension popup (approx 380px wide, rounded 12px, dark theme,
  subtle 1px border, layered soft shadow) floating over a blurred Chrome window with a messy tab strip.
- No fake browser chrome text like "Google Chrome" logos; no third-party logos except a tiny neutral
  "OpenAI · Anthropic · Gemini · Ollama" text row on frame 4. No people, no hands, no stock photos.
- Every frame: the Shelve icon at 40px in the top-left with the word "Shelve" next to it in silver.
- Copy must be spelled exactly as written below. No extra taglines, no lorem ipsum, no emoji.

## Frames

### 1 — hero.png  "One click. Every tab where it belongs."
Subhead: "Shelve reads your open tabs and sorts them into named Chrome tab groups by intent — not by website."
Mockup: a chaotic strip of 30 tiny favicons at the top of the browser dissolving into five collapsed,
colour-coded Chrome tab groups in the popup: "Apartment hunt" (blue, 6), "Interview prep" (green, 5),
"Transformer papers" (purple, 4), "Weekend trip" (orange, 4), "Read later" (grey, 3). A single primary
button at the bottom of the popup reads "Organize".

### 2 — command-bar.png  "Just say it."
Subhead: "Group, rename, merge, jump, or ask — in plain English."
Mockup: the popup with a command bar input at the top containing the typed text
`open the hotel under $200 a night` and, below it, a result line “Jumped to ‘Canal-side apartment in Hackney’”.
Around the popup, four floating pill-shaped example commands in silver on graphite:
"group my arxiv tabs called Papers" · "make the news group red" · "close dupes" · "which listing allows pets?"

### 3 — shelve-resume.png  "Put a project down. Pick it up later."
Subhead: "Shelve a group and its tabs close behind an AI brief of where you left off."
Mockup: popup showing a "Shelved" list with one expanded card titled "Apartment hunt · 6 tabs · 2 days ago"
and a brief paragraph: "You'd narrowed it to two flats in Hackney under £1,900. The Islington one had no
balcony; you hadn't checked Southbank's move-in date yet." Buttons: "Resume" (primary) and "Delete".

### 4 — private.png  "Your keys. Your tabs. Your rules."
Subhead: "Bring your own OpenAI, Anthropic, Gemini, or local Ollama key — or use the free tier with no signup. No analytics, no tracking."
Mockup: the Settings page (wider, ~560px) with a "Provider" select showing "Anthropic", a masked API key field
"sk-ant-••••••••••", a "Budget cap" field "$1.00", and a "Command routing" select showing "TypeSafe Jev — fast typed decisions".
Small neutral text row under the mockup: "OpenAI · Anthropic · Gemini · Ollama · Shelve Free".

### 5 — stats.png  "Watch the clutter go."
Subhead: "Tabs wrangled, projects shelved, and your streak — counted on your machine only."
Mockup: popup with a stats card: "1,284 tabs wrangled", "37 projects shelved", "12-day streak", plus a row of
quick-action buttons "Merge windows · Close duplicates · Ungroup all · Undo".
